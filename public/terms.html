Terms

ConnectorDome helps translate external observations into ASF-compatible events. It is provided as-is under Apache-2.0.

CONFORMANCE_PASS is not a safety certification.

A CONFORMANCE_PASS means the connector satisfied defined ConnectorDome/ASF mechanical checks. It does not mean:

  • the sensor is accurate
  • the upstream API is truthful
  • the data is complete
  • the connector is safe for every environment
  • the device is calibrated
  • the observation is authoritative
  • the connector is fit for life-safety use

ConnectorDome does not certify upstream data as true. Observation never implies authority.

Source data belongs to its upstream owner/publisher. ConnectorDome compatibility does not imply upstream endorsement. A connector that reads a public weather API does not mean that agency endorses ConnectorDome unless that agency publishes or endorses the connector.

You remain responsible for having authority to access and expose a source, including sources that may contain personal or confidential information.

Guest use requires no account. Public registry publication, when added, will be opt-in.
